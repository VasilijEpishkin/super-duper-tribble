# Design Direction

## Direction name
Molten Relic Brutalism

## Summary
An immersive, dark editorial environment that presents handcrafted jewelry as tactile personal artifacts. This is the current Stitch direction, pending user approval for the website.

## Design language
- Mood: Dark romanticism, quiet intensity, mystery, subversive elegance.
- Composition: Asymmetric editorial arrangements with deliberate negative space.
- Visual density: Sparse, artifact-led.
- Typography feel: Mechanical Courier Prime paired with readable Geist.
- Color feel: Obsidian black, raw earth, restrained crimson, silver and white.
- Material/effects: Chiseled hairlines and tonal layering; actual jewelry imagery is central.
- Motion feel: Restrained; respect reduced-motion preferences.

## References / Design DNA
- References used: Stitch project “Altar Redesign System”; Miro ALTAR’ board.
- DNA source file: DESIGN.md
- Key patterns: Sharp geometry, monospaced labels, expansive black fields, artifact photography.

## Layout principles
- Grid/container: 12 columns desktop; 4 columns mobile.
- Section rhythm: Spacious vertical pauses and edge-anchored utility details.
- Card/layout patterns: Sharp rectangular frames and asymmetric editorial image/text groupings.
- Responsive behavior: Keep type, contrast, and product facts readable on narrow screens.

## Do / Avoid
### Do
- Let verified product photography and story carry the experience.
- Pair lyrical brand language with clear specifications and actions.
- Keep crimson for sparse, meaningful emphasis.

### Avoid
- Rounded cards, pill controls, generic luxury templates, invented product details, and unverified claims.
