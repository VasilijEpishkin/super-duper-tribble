# Success Criteria

## Product discovery
- [ ] Primary conversion and first-release scope are approved.
- [ ] Product facts and customer policies are verified.
- [ ] The exact Stitch screens to implement are selected.

## UX and visual
- [ ] Users can understand the brand and reach the primary action quickly.
- [ ] Product story and verified specifications are both easy to find.
- [ ] The approved dark editorial visual direction is consistent and responsive.
- [ ] Keyboard operation, contrast, focus states, and reduced motion are covered.

## Technical
- [ ] The chosen stack installs and builds.
- [ ] Core flows work at mobile and desktop breakpoints.
- [ ] Browser console and accessibility checks are reviewed.
- [ ] Runtime evidence is saved in qa-evidence.md.
