# Finish gate — ALTAR’ multi-page preview

## Direction

Editorial Mineral, based on ALTAR’ source photography and the approved Stitch system: warm paper, charcoal, restrained oxblood, EB Garamond and a neutral sans. The homepage uses five distinct images; catalog cards use separate images. Page layouts vary by purpose rather than repeating a single landing-page template.

## Review

- Every page has a clear heading and next action.
- Navigation stays inside the new site; no storefront links return to the old site.
- Product names and prices are source-listed. Checkout, shipping, stock, warranty and availability are not presented as live facts.
- Reduced-motion, visible keyboard focus, skip link, descriptive image alternatives and mobile layouts are present.
- Product-specific image mapping still needs brand review before commerce launch.

## Before a real store launch

- Confirm contact details and connect the inquiry route.
- Verify product image-to-SKU mapping and current availability.
- Connect payment, inventory and order handoff; confirm legal copy and shipping policies.
- Review the authenticated private preview at desktop and mobile sizes.
