# ALTAR’ website — discovery brief v0

## Product
Russian-language digital storefront and editorial home for ALTAR’, an independent Moscow jewelry brand making handcrafted silver objects with personal symbolic meaning.

## Audience
People drawn to sculptural handmade jewelry, self-expression, and personal symbolism. Miro describes a feminine strength and sacred beauty, while explicitly avoiding literal esotericism, a witch/goddess character, aggression, overt sexuality, or demonstrative luxury.

## Goal
Help visitors discover pieces, understand their meaning and craft, and purchase an available item. Preserve the current catalog, product-size selection, cart, and checkout journey as the baseline. Individual commissions remain a secondary conversion through Telegram.

## First-release scope
- Home / editorial entry and featured pieces.
- Jewelry catalog and product detail with verified photos, price, size/material facts, story, and purchase action.
- Cart and checkout continuation using the existing commerce service once its integration is confirmed.
- Individual-order information and Telegram contact route.
- Workshop story, jewelry care / warranty guidance, and contact page.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts; Russian copy by default.

## Evidence from the live site (2026-10-03)
- Homepage promotes best sellers, the handmade process, a first-order discount, cart, and direct purchase.
- Product pages show price, SKU, selectable ring sizes, purchase action, and an individual product story.
- Current featured examples include four pieces priced from ₽10,000 to ₽14,000.
- Recommendations page says pieces are 925 silver and describes care and a lifetime repair/polish warranty. Confirm these claims with the brand before reusing them.
- Custom-order page invites ideas for jewelry and other silver objects, with a Telegram CTA.
- Workshop, recommendations, and contacts are existing content areas.
- In the observed browser session, the catalog page displayed a solid red content area with no accessible product entries; one product detail showed empty image placeholders. Treat these as current-site defects to investigate, not desired design.

## Brand and references
- Primary sources: Miro ALTAR’ philosophy/packaging boards; Stitch project “Altar Redesign System”; live website https://altarishere.com/.
- Existing Stitch design system is named “Molten Relic Brutalism”; it is a style source, not an approved page design.
- Use real artifact photography and restrained, tactile editorial composition. Keep room for interpretation and pair poetic meaning with clear product facts.

## Decisions still required before implementation
- Confirm access/export path for the complete product catalog, current photos, stock, and size availability.
- Confirm commerce platform and whether checkout must remain in Tilda or may be replaced; do not invent payment or order behavior.
- Verify shipping, returns, privacy/consent copy, warranty details, and discount behavior.
- Confirm whether Russian/Moscow remains the sole market and language for release.
- Review the Stitch screens after generation and request visual changes if needed.
- Select stack and deployment target after commerce constraints are known.

## Quality target
Polished production storefront with high visual craft, readable product information, accessible keyboard/focus behavior, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
