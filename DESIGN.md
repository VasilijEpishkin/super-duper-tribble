## Brand & Style

### Brand Philosophy & Emotional Core
This design system embodies Dark Romanticism, Surreal Avant-Garde, Molten Sculpturalism, and Subversive Elegance. It does not treat jewelry as mere adornment, but as visceral relics, talismans of personal power, inner friction, and raw sacred rites. The interface operates as an uncompromised ritual altar: quiet, dangerous, stark, and undeniably tactile.

### Target Audience & Persona
Created for vanguard aesthetes, avant-garde collectors, and individuals who reject sanitized commercial luxury in favor of unpolished truth, brutalist architecture, handcrafted metals, and dark erotic poetry.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
A radical fusion of **Gothic Avant-Garde Minimal Brutalism** and **Tactile Molten Skeuomorphism**:
- **Void Obsidian Canvas:** Expansive fields of absolute black (`#000000`) and damp earth espresso (`#2C1900`) create an abyss that elevates silver, mineral stones, and blood-red accents into holy artifacts.
- **Relic Typography:** Monospaced and mechanical typewriter letterforms evoke sacred transcripts, manifestos, and clinical-yet-occult cataloging.
- **Molten & Chiseled Geometries:** Razor-sharp perimeter boundaries (`border-radius: 0px`) frame high-contrast macro-photography of organic, molten metals, dripping wax, velvet linings, and raw gemstone prongs.

## Colors

### The Ritual Hierarchy
The color architecture rejects standard SaaS tints in favor of visceral, symbolic tonal layers:

- **Primary (`#9C0000` / Crimson Energy):** Reserved for vital signifiers—critical calls to action, sacred selection rings, sacrificial badges, and active state indicators. It carries the weight of dried and arterial blood.
- **Secondary (`#2C1900` / Raw Earth):** A deep espresso brown representing wet peat, burnt timber, and oxidized casting molds. Used in card backdrops, subtle section breaks, and deep tactile depths.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#000000` / Deep Void Black):** Absolute black canvas that swallows background distractions, ensuring molten silver and iridescent gemstones emerge with photographic drama.
- **Text & Light Contrast (`#FFFFFF` / Pure White):** High-contrast, clinical white for editorial text, tabular specifications, and unyielding glyphs.
- **Metallic Tiers (`#D8D8D8` Molten Silver & `#E5E5E5` Liquid Mercury):** Structural lines, razor-thin borders, and metallic specular accents reminiscent of beaten sterling silver plates and sacred trays.

## Typography

### Conceptual Tension
The typography pairs harsh, manual, typewriter transcript aesthetics (**Courier Prime**) with precise, hyper-rational modernist sans-serif (**Geist**). This duality references both typewriter-manifesto art cards enclosed in molten metal boxes and the clean, high-precision cataloging required for high-jewelry appraisal.

### Hierarchical Directives
- **Manifestos & Display (`display-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Executed in uppercase or raw casing with Courier Prime. Used for uncompromising proclamations, relic numbers, and philosophical stances.
- **Editorial & Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Set in Geist with relaxed line-heights and subtle letter spacing. Ensures effortless reading of intimate lore, metal compositions, gemstone origins, and care rites.
- **Alchemical Data Labels (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`):** Always uppercase, tracked wide (`0.12em` to `0.22em`). Used for metallurgical purity marks (`925 STERLING SILVER`), carat specs, inventory tokens, and interactive button cues.

## Layout & Spacing

### Editorial Canvas Rhythm
The design uses an asymmetric, spacious layout that treats screen real estate like an avant-garde exhibition room:
- **Columns & Framing:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`) collapsing to a 4-column structured stack on mobile (`margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`).
- **Void Space Ratios:** Expansive vertical pauses (`space-xl` and above) allow singular artifacts—a solitary molten ring or a sculptural pendant—to command complete meditative focus.
- **Architectural Edge Anchors:** Structural information (serial codes, cart counters, brand marks) is pinned tight to the viewport perimeters, leaving the inner core open for tactile imagery and text manifestos.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification & Chiseled Silver Borders
Traditional software dropshadows are forbidden. Depth is achieved purely through tactile physical metaphors:
1. **The Abyss (Ground Zero):** `#000000` void base canvas.
2. **The Excavation Layer (Surface 1):** `#110b05` / `#190d00` (deepest raw earth) with razor-sharp 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **The Crucible Altar (Surface 2 / Cards):** `#2c1900` blended into `#0f0f0f` gradient layers, bordered with 1px solid `#332211` or brushed metallic outlines (`rgba(216, 216, 216, 0.2)`).
4. **Sacred Focus & Modal Elevation:** Dark room velvet overlays with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and low-opacity crimson glow reflections (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px rgba(156, 0, 0, 0.25)`).
5. **Brushed Specular Highlights:** Interactive elements feature an ultra-thin 1px top highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) replicating light catching the sharp rim of an antique silver serving tray.

## Shapes

### Zero-Radius Brutalist Architecture
All UI elements adhere strictly to **`roundedness: 0` (Sharp 0px)**. 
- No soft corners, pills, or rounded cards exist in the interface.
- Buttons, modal overlays, image frames, input containers, and tags are machined with unyielding, razor-edged 90-degree corners.
- This extreme structural rigidity acts as a stark counterpoint to the organic, flowing, liquid-sculptural forms of the molten jewelry itself.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Offering Button:** Sharp rectangle, background `#9C0000`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-lg` in uppercase. Hover transitions to `#BE0D0D` with an inner 1px border of `rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Silver Relic Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#D8D8D8`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover triggers an inverse flash: background `#FFFFFF`, text `#000000`.
- **Ghost / Rite Button:** Underlined monospaced text with `label-md`, offset 4px, hover color shifting from `#FFFFFF` to `#9C0000`.

### Cards (Product & Relic Showcases)
- Zero-radius container with background `#0A0703` (subtle raw earth tint) framed by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Image presentation features dramatic photographic crops on raw velvet, unpolished cardboard, or brushed steel.
- Hover action initiates an eerie, slow 1.03x image zoom with a hairline border transformation from muted charcoal to oxidized crimson (`#9C0000`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Solid `#000000` background with a mandatory bottom-only 1px border of `#443322`.
- Focus state transforms the border to 1px solid `#9C0000` with uppercase typewriter labels floating above in `label-sm`.
- Caret is rendered as an unblinking block or sharp crimson line.

### Checkboxes & Radio Indicators
- Sharp square boxes (16px x 16px) with 1px border `#D8D8D8`.
- Checked state fills the interior with a solid crimson square (`#9C0000`) or an engraved "X" typewriter glyph.
- Radio buttons utilize concentric sharp diamonds (rotated 45-degree squares) rather than circles.

### Chips, Badges & Artifact Tags
- Monospaced metadata tags (`label-sm`), strictly uppercase, encased in 1px borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` with black backgrounds.
- Used for displaying metallurgy codes (`AG 925`, `RAW SAPPHIRE`, `MOLTEN 1/1`).

### Custom Product Specimen Details
- **The Relic Manifest Drawer:** An accordion panel detailing the talisman’s energetic symbol, handcrafted molten weight, and casting lore, styled with dotted tab leaders (`....`) typical of antique collector registers.
