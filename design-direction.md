# ALTAR’ website — visual direction proposal

Status: The first light “Editorial Mineral” implementation was rejected as visually weak and insufficiently faithful to Stitch. This document now records the revised direction for the next design pass; it is not an approval of the current site.

## Source constraints
Miro describes a woman whose strength is self-possessed, whose softness has inner support, and whose personal symbol can mean something different to each wearer. It explicitly rejects demonstrative luxury. Use the existing Miro and Stitch photographs as the visual source. Do not reproduce literal occult imagery, a witch/goddess character, aggression, or overt sexuality. The current Stitch system provides a dark editorial foundation, but its “ritual/erotic” wording is not an instruction for the storefront.

## Direction — Living Artifact
Treat each real ALTAR’ piece as the protagonist. Use the 12 verified product-photo pairs in `stitch-content-audit.md`; reserve wider editorial images for brand sections only when their subject and provenance are checked. Compose the first viewport as an exhibition opening: one decisive product photograph, a short brand statement from Miro, and one clear catalog action. Vary scale and pacing across pages with full-bleed images, close details, and quiet factual captions. Avoid the current sequence of generic pale sections and repetitive equal-width cards outside the shopping grid.

Warm mineral-white, charcoal and restrained oxblood remain a starting palette, while typography and page rhythm need fresh visual review in Stitch. Product names, prices and actions stay clear in the catalog. Give home, catalog, product, atelier and contacts their own compositions; connect them through the same image treatment, navigation and motion language.

The existing Stitch screens are drafts, not approved implementation references: their exports include invented products and claims. Correct the saved screens with the verified product/photo map before transferring them. Exclude decorative occult/religious symbols and invented jewelry photography or claims. The current implementation is a visual prototype, not a functioning commerce system.

## Interaction principles
- Navigation and cart stay immediately discoverable.
- Product price, sizes, material facts, and purchase action are clear without scrolling past long brand copy.
- Keep custom-order contact distinct from ready-to-buy checkout.
- Use motion to articulate entry, browsing and page changes: a short crossfade between routes, staggered section reveals, and a restrained image/arrow response on product hover. Animate opacity and transform; stop all nonessential motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Treat these prototype effects as a baseline. The revised Stitch screens must specify the intended interaction for each key component before the final transfer.
