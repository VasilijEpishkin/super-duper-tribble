# ALTAR’ website — success criteria (proposed)

## Discovery gate
- [x] User approved the Stitch-first workflow, Miro/Stitch source use, and all-page first design pass.
- [ ] Complete catalog/photos and product facts are available and verified.
- [ ] Commerce provider, checkout handoff, and deployment constraints are confirmed.
- [ ] Shipping, returns, privacy, and warranty content are approved.

## Experience
- [ ] A visitor can reach the catalog from the home page and understand the brand quickly.
- [ ] Product pages show usable imagery, verified price/material/size facts, story, and a clear purchase action.
- [ ] Ready-to-buy and custom-order journeys are distinct and functional.
- [ ] All current site sections in the approved scope have a clear route and useful content.
- [ ] Desktop and mobile layouts remain readable and usable; keyboard focus and reduced motion are covered.

## Delivery evidence
- [ ] Stitch screens cover all pages in `page-map.md`; home, catalog, and product detail have desktop and mobile counterparts.
- [ ] Build runs in the selected stack and commerce handoff works in a real browser.
- [ ] Browser console, responsive behavior, and accessibility basics are reviewed with evidence recorded in `qa-evidence.md`.
